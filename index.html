See a worked example (Technical — Handling)
Outcome goal: I want to improve my ability to catch crosses cleanly under pressure.
Stop: Punching or parrying balls I could catch cleanly.
Start: Attacking the ball at its highest point with two hands.
Continue: Practising catching technique with balls of different heights and speeds.
Weeks 1–4: I catch the ball cleanly in unopposed catching drills.
Weeks 5–16: I catch crosses cleanly with one attacker challenging me.
Weeks 17+: I catch crosses cleanly in full-pressure match situations.
